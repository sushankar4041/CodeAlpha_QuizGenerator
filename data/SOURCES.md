- **Source**: CAIS (Center for AI Safety) / Hugging Face (`cais/mmlu`)

- **License**: MIT License / CC BY 4.0
- **Redistribution Permission**: Permitted for commercial and non-commercial open-source applications.
- **Attribution**: "Hendrycks et al., Measuring Massive Multitask Language Understanding (MMLU), 2021."

- **Source**: Open-Source Computer Science & Web Engineering Repositories (MIT License)
